What's new in 0.1.0 — first public release:
- AI 3D-printing engineer right inside Orca Slicer.
- Slicer context: model on the plate and printer / filament / print-setting profiles.
- Context checkboxes and preset export mode (changed only / all), remembered per chat.
- Multi-chat with local history, search and pinning.
- Streaming responses, stop, regenerate, edit and resend.
- Photos (up to 6 MB) and text files (up to 1 MB) as attachments.
- 10 built-in providers plus custom OpenAI/Anthropic-compatible ones.
- Settings in 4 tabs with per-model temperature, max tokens and reasoning mode.
- Commands, localization (EN/RU/SR) and themes (auto / white / black).
